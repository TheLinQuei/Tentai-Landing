How it works

From request to delivery

1 · Tell us what you need. A list of items and quantities, or just the job — “equip a 20-person office”, “a server for our school” — and we'll specify it.

2 · Get one clear quote. Itemized prices, delivery to your town, the lead time, and exactly who handles customs clearance, duty and VAT — so the landed cost holds no surprises.

3 · We source and ship. Equipment is bought new through established IT distributors — never grey-market — and serial numbers are recorded on your invoice.

4 · Set up, if you want it. Machines can be configured before they ship — accounts, software, security settings — or set up remotely once they arrive.
