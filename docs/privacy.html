Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-17 · Effective: 2026-05-17 · Tentai Technology operating since 2023

Notice: This document is a working draft and has not yet been reviewed by counsel. The substantive commitments below are accurate to our current implementation; legal phrasing may be revised before the public beta opens. If you have questions in the meantime, email shykem.middleton@tentaitech.com.

1. Who we are

Tentai Technology (operator: Shykem Middleton) builds Vi (AI assistant) and delivers client services — websites, booking automation, and AI integrations. This policy covers all of them collectively, unless a product-specific notice supersedes it.

2. Data we collect

  • Account data — email address (sign-in), display name, optional canonical name/aliases.
  • Conversation data — messages you send Vi and Vi's replies, including timestamps and which model produced each reply.
  • Derived memory — facts and episodes Vi extracts from your conversations so she can remember you across sessions.
  • Per-turn diagnostics — model selection, latency, content-classifier signals (internal debugging only).
  • Idle state — Vi's autonomy queue and repo digests if you've enabled the dev integration.

We do not collect: voice recordings, payment information, location data beyond what you tell Vi conversationally, advertising identifiers, or biometric data.

3. How we use it

Your data is used by Vi, for you, and stays inside Tentai's infrastructure. We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it to train external AI models.

4. Where it's stored

PostgreSQL on Google Cloud SQL, region us-central1 (Iowa, USA). Encrypted at rest by Google Cloud Platform. Backed up daily. Access is restricted to the operator and authorized service accounts.

5. Your rights (GDPR Articles 15 + 17)

Regardless of jurisdiction, every user has the right to access and to erasure.

  • Export — POST /me/data-export on the Vi API returns a one-hour download URL containing every per-user record we hold about you (password hashes and session tokens are never included).
  • Delete — POST /me/data-delete with body { "confirmation": "DELETE MY DATA" } permanently removes every per-user row. Deletions are immediate at the application layer; Google Cloud backup retention may retain copies for up to 7 days.

6. Retention

  • Active accounts: data retained indefinitely until you exercise erasure.
  • Deleted accounts: purged immediately; no soft-delete tombstone.
  • Backups: Google Cloud SQL automated backup retention (currently 7 days) may include deleted data for that window.

7. Cookies

One session cookie: vi_session. HTTP-only, Secure-flagged. Cleared automatically on sign-out or account deletion. We do not use analytics, tracking, or advertising cookies.

8. Children

Vi is not designed for users under 13 (US COPPA) or under 16 (EU GDPR without parental consent). We do not knowingly accept signups from minors.

9. Changes to this policy

Material changes will be announced on the landing page and via push notification (if you've opted in) at least 14 days before taking effect.
