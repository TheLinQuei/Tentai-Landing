Sol Calendar

Tentai's internal calendar — 13 months of 28 days each, plus a Still Day at year-end. Anchored to Gregorian (Sol Day 1 = Jan 1), so the year boundary stays familiar even though every month is exactly four weeks.

Pick your birthday, anniversary, or any date to see when it falls in Sol Calendar.
