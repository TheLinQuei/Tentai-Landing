About Tentai Technology

Tentai Technology is a solo-built AI and software studio. Vi is the flagship — a continuous AI with real memory, the same self across web and mobile. The same discipline goes into every client build.

Every Tentai product is built without tracking, ads, or harvested data. Your conversations, your schedules, your state — they belong to you. That's not a policy bolted on afterward; it's the architecture.

Vi is live. Client work is open now — websites, booking automation, and AI integrations. If you have a project in mind, reach out.

Shykem

Founder & Sole Engineer · Tentai Technology

Tentai is a solo build. Every line of code, every persona decision, every brand mark on this page comes from one person with one vision: tools that feel continuous — that remember you, that don't forget who you are between sessions, that respect your attention instead of farming it.

Vi started as an experiment in giving an AI real memory and a real identity instead of treating it as a stateless answer machine. Client work followed — real businesses needed the same things Tentai was already building for itself.

The throughline: build what should exist, and ship it with the discipline a team of fifty would have.
