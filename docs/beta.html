What it is

An AI companion that remembers you, in the character you give it.

Vi is built by Tentai. As a tester you get your own, running on the same system: you choose its name and who it is.

It remembers you

Tell it something once and it carries it forward: your name, your people, what you were dealing with last week. You are not reintroducing yourself every time you open the app.

You write who it is

Pick a name. Start from one of our personalities, or describe its character yourself in plain words. Warm, dry, blunt or playful: that is your call, not ours.

Our updates do not rewrite it

The character you choose is copied into your account, not pointed at a template we can edit, so changing our templates never touches it. The AI model underneath can still change while we test, so tell us if yours ever stops sounding like itself.

The one you talk to should still be there tomorrow.

What we ask

Four small things

  • A short survey

    A few questions when you apply, about two minutes. It helps us pick a real mix of people, not just whoever clicked first.

  • Honest feedback

    Tell us what breaks, what feels off, and what would make you stay. Blunt is welcome. Polite and vague does not help anyone.

  • 18 or older

    The beta is for adults only. You confirm your age when you sign up.

  • Patience

    It is a beta. Some things will break, and some features are still being built. Tell us when they do.
