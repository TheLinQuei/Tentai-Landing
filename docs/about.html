About Tentai Technology

An independent AI & software studio — building calm, continuous tools, and the products behind them.

Tentai Technology is an independent AI and software studio, founded in 2023 and based in the United States. We design and operate our own AI products — and bring the same engineering to businesses that need custom software.

Our flagship is Vi: a continuous AI assistant with real, persistent memory — the same identity across web, mobile, and Discord. Vigil extends that presence into Discord communities. Both are our own products, built and maintained in-house rather than assembled from off-the-shelf parts.

For clients, we build the same way we build for ourselves: custom websites, booking and scheduling automation, and AI integrations — shipped with full code ownership, no tracking, and no platform lock-in.

Every Tentai product is built without tracking, ads, or harvested data. Your conversations, your schedules, your state belong to you. That's not a policy bolted on afterward — it's the architecture.

Tentai is a solo build. Every line of code, every persona decision, and every brand mark comes from one person with one vision: tools that feel continuous — that remember you, that don't forget who you are between sessions, that respect your attention instead of farming it.

Vi began as an experiment in giving an AI real memory and a real identity instead of a stateless answer machine. Vigil followed, so the same presence could reach Discord communities without becoming a different bot. Client work came next — real businesses needed exactly what Tentai was already building for itself.
