About Tentai Technology

An independent technology company — an AI you make your own, software you keep, and the hardware to run it.

Tentai Technology is an independent technology company, founded in 2023 and based in the United States. We build an AI people make their own, custom software for businesses, and source the hardware to run it.

Our product is an AI that's actually yours. You give it a name and decide who it is, and it remembers you from one conversation to the next. The character you write is saved to your account, so our updates don't rewrite it. It's in a closed beta now.

Vi, pictured, is ours: the first AI built on this system, made and maintained in-house rather than assembled from off-the-shelf parts. She's also who answers when you chat with us on this site.

For clients, we build the same way we build for ourselves: custom websites, booking and scheduling automation, and AI integrations — shipped with full code ownership, no tracking, and no platform lock-in. When the job needs machines, we quote, source and ship those too.

Every Tentai product is built without tracking, ads, or harvested data. Your conversations, your schedules, your state belong to you. That's not a policy bolted on afterward — it's the architecture.

Tentai is a solo build. Every line of code, every persona decision, and every brand mark comes from one person with one vision: tools that feel continuous — that remember you, that don't forget who you are between sessions, that respect your attention instead of farming it.

Vi began as an experiment in giving an AI real memory and a real identity instead of a stateless answer machine. Once it worked, the next step was obvious: let people make their own. Client work and hardware came alongside — real businesses needed the same engineering, and the machines to run it.
