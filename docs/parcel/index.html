Parcels on the books

Take in a parcel

Try it: take a parcel in and it gets its own number. Tap On the bus, then Arrived, and the message to the receiver writes itself. Then switch to Customer at the top and type that number in — that is what stops the phone calls.

This is the page your customer opens. No app to download, no account, no phone call — they type the number from their receipt and see exactly where their parcel is.
